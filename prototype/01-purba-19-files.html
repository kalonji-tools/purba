Assigning actors with gitattributes syntax

The question: does the established gitattributes language feel right for binding a reader to a location, written to a purba-owned filename so git never reads it? Everything below runs on purba's 19 real tracked files. Nothing persists; reload to reset.

Eleven assignable actors. Dashed are groups that expand. Click to insert.

Bindings

gitattributes syntax. pattern actor, -actor to stop inheritance, # comments.

Resolution
